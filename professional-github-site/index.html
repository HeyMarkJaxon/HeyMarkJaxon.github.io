About

Security is strongest when people can understand and operate it.

I am completing an Associate of Science in Cybersecurity and Network Engineering Technology with a focus on cloud infrastructure security. My work spans Windows and Linux administration, packet analysis, enterprise policy, automation, network security, and secure infrastructure.

I am especially interested in identity: how users, devices, applications, and services prove who they are, what they are allowed to access, and how those decisions can be made securely at enterprise scale. My next professional step is to deepen my experience in IAM, Microsoft 365, Entra ID, and cloud security while continuing to build a strong systems and networking foundation.

Long term, I want to become a trusted technical expert and independent consultant capable of working across complex, international environments.
